## Protocol (D050)

1. **Teacher.** Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 label a 600-text pilot pool focused on the weak tags. Two agent readers, blind to model names and votes, read every (text, tag) pair where the three disagree. The teacher with the highest accuracy wins; Sonnet if within 5 points of Opus. Gold is not used.
2. **Relabel and retrain.** The chosen teacher relabels a pool around the weak tags (`neurosurgery`, `ob_delivery`, `burns`, `trauma`, `icu`, `ventilator`, `cath_lab`). Three students train on the merged labels: 1b (the WP4 student code), 2a (frozen embeddings plus logistic regression), 2b (a fine-tuned small encoder).
3. **Ship rule, per tag.** A candidate replaces the current method on a tag only if (1) its paired 95% bootstrap interval of gold F1 minus the current method's gold F1 lies entirely above 0, and (2) a blinded hand read of 25 random texts where it disagrees with the current method, by an agent that did not build it, finds at least 20 of 25 in the candidate's favour. If several candidates pass, the tie-break is validation F1 against teacher labels, not gold.
4. **Budget and freeze.** At most $12 for this run; outputs are candidate files only.

## How firm the gold gate is

Disclosure only. The gate is the declared interval (seed 20261122, 2,000 stratified resamples, committed in `scripts/wp4_student.py` before D050, see `seed_provenance` in decision.json); it was not rerun with another seed, and nothing below feeds the decision. ## Disclosures

- The `ob_delivery` gold gate passes at the declared seed (+0.0050 to +0.1438; seed 20261122, 2,000 resamples, committed in scripts/wp4_student.py before D050), but only just. Run to 50,000 resamples, the same resampling puts the lower bound at +0.0012, with P(gain <= 0) = 2.3% (Monte Carlo SE 0.1%). Across 100 other seeds, each running the declared 2,000-resample procedure, the lower bound ranged from -0.0035 to +0.0079 (median +0.0012) and was above 0 in 70 of 100; the declared run's lower bound is higher than 95 of them. Pooled over those 200,000 resamples: +0.0013 to +0.1437, P(gain <= 0) = 2.3%. The candidate mix's macro gain touches 0 the same way: +0.0002 to +0.0060 declared, +0.0000 to +0.0060 at 50,000 resamples, P(gain <= 0) = 2.3%. The gold evidence is marginal; the change rests mainly on the blinded read (23 of 25, 20 to 25 if every hard call flipped). The decision is unchanged and the gate was not rerun with another seed: D050 fixes the declared interval. The integration pass should weigh this before promoting.
- Gold decides the first gate per tag (as in WP4), so the candidate mix's gold F1 is slightly optimistic. 3 candidates x 24 tags = 72 paired tests were run. At a 2.5% one-sided rate, up to about 2 passes could come from chance alone if no candidate were better and the tests were independent (many are neither: several tags have no gold error to fix). 2 passed. The hand read, which does not use gold, is what tells a real gain from a lucky one: it passed 1 and failed 1.
- Gold labels and hand reads are agent-made, not clinicians' readings. The 777 gold texts were labeled by four agent labelers who split on specialty names in lists.
- Reader independence: D050 asks for a reader that did not build the candidate. No sidecar names the reader, so the files cannot show who read; the workflow assigned the reads to separate reader agents. The blind files hold only item id, text and tag, in shuffled order, but their names carry the candidate id and tag (e.g. `pop_blind_ob_delivery_2b_r2.csv`), and the key (`pop_key.csv`, which holds both taggers' calls) sits in the same folder. What the files do show: every read file was written once, before its unblinding, and is unchanged since (sha256 equal): pilot reads at 04:52 and 04:53 UTC against unblinding at 04:55; population reads at 05:22 and 05:24 against the first decide run at 05:45.
- Teacher prompt: gold texts are in it. The frozen WP3 p2 prompt (sha256 44665b50), reused unchanged as D050 required, has 1 labeled example equal to a gold text after normalization ('empanelled under ayushman bharat - pm-jay' -> ['scheme'], gold 'empanelled under ayushman bharat pm-jay') and 2 that contain a gold text ('orthopedic and trauma surgeries conducted in operation th...' contains gold 'trauma surgeries'; 'has a dedicated burn unit' contains gold 'dedicated burn unit'). Their gold tags are `burns`, `emergency`, `scheme`, `trauma`; none involves `ob_delivery`, the only tag that changes. `burns` passed the gold gate but not the hand read, so no decision rests on it. 6 more prompt facts (labeled examples or facts quoted in the rules) contain a one-word gold text (angioplasty, cpap, ventilator); a bare tag word is not counted as a copy. The earlier statement that no gold text is in any prompt was wrong.
- Gold separation under looser keys: under the declared near-duplicate rule, 0 of the 36,299 merged-label texts and 0 of the 50 population draws equal a gold text. Looser keys find some: 8 merged texts equal a gold text on letters and digits only (7 in training; e.g. 'neuro critical care' (claude-opus-5-5) against gold 'neurocritical care'), and 33 have the same set of words as a gold text (27 in training). None of these gold texts is among the 14 gold rows the mix changes. No population draw matches under either looser key. 6 of the 11 gold rows the mix fixes on `ob_delivery` have a claude-opus-5-5-labeled training text with a difflib ratio of 0.85 to 0.94 (templated text, e.g. 'cesarean delivery is provided' against gold 'cesarean delivery is performed'), and the teacher's label on that text equals the gold label in 6 of 6. Part of the gold gain may come from near copies rather than from generalising, one more reason to rest the change on the population read.
- Validation texts: WP4's 5,859 validation texts minus 2 near-gold texts dropped at the merge ('center of excellence includes accident & emerge...'; 'ventilator.'), so 5,857. merged_labels.parquet's own split column marks 1,389 further texts as validation; the students trained on them, and the column is not used anywhere in WP4b scoring.
- The hand-read draw does not exclude texts the candidate trained on (D050 does not ask it to). Results split by whether a drawn text is in the merged teacher labels are in decision.json.
- Validation F1 (the tie-break) measures agreement with teacher labels, on thresholds tuned on the same texts; it was not needed this run because no tag had two passing candidates.
- Student 2b trains on the Apple MPS GPU, which is not bit-for-bit deterministic. The candidate mix uses the saved candidate tags (sha256 in the sidecar); a retrain can differ on a few texts.
- 25 texts per (candidate, tag) is a small read: 23 of 25 has a Wilson 95% interval of about 75% to 98%.
- The gold set over-draws regex hits and near misses. Most of a candidate's changes fall on texts the gold set does not resemble; the population read is the only check on those.

## For future runs

Not changed in this run (they would alter frozen inputs or the protocol), recorded so the next run avoids them:

- Near-duplicate rule: key on letters and digits only (and consider same-word-set equality) at the merge stage and in the draw exclusion, not only lowercase with punctuation dropped.
- Teacher prompt: exclude gold texts from any future prompt revision; check examples against gold before freezing.
- Blind files: give them neutral names (for example pop_blind_<random>.csv) and keep the key in a folder readers are not given.
- Reader provenance: each reader writes a sidecar with its agent or session id, start and end time, and the files it opened.
- merged_labels.parquet: drop or rename its split column in a future merge; it is unused and differs from the split the students used (this stage does not own that file).
