# Overnight test and fix round

Five testers used the app on Nov 22, 02:56 to 03:30 ET: Hruday's demo rehearsal, an ASHA worker on a phone, a skeptical judge, a QA edge-case tester and a numbers auditor. This round fixed every high and medium issue it could fix inside its allowed files (`app/`, `dbapp/server.py`, `tests/`, and wording or bug fixes in `src/vf/`). Gates, windows, recipes, flags and ordering rules were not changed. Issues that would need one of those changes, or a file outside scope, are listed as open.

## Result

- 9 high issues (after merging duplicates across testers): 7 fixed, 2 partly fixed with an open step for Hruday.
- 27 medium issues (after merging): 21 fixed, 3 partly fixed, 3 open or rejected with reasons.
- Low issues: 14 fixed, the rest listed as open.
- Front end: `pnpm typecheck` clean, `pnpm build` ok (`dbapp/static/assets/index-COzRBfmM.js`).
- Bundle: `scripts/wp12_bundle.py` rebuilt it (needed for the phone-mask fix in citations), and `--check` says ok: dbapp/vf matches src/vf, 15 of 15 files, no file over 10 MB, no call number in clear.
- Tests: 1,121 passed, 9 skipped, 1 xfailed (`pytest -q`). Two tests in `tests/test_server.py` were updated for the new unknown-service sentence. `tests/test_overnight_fixes.py` adds 8 regression tests.
- Every fix below was checked in the browser on port 8920 (Playwright, its own context, 1440x900 or 390x844). Screenshots are `reports/figures/overnight/fixed_*.png` (24 files). No console errors on any checked page, except the 400s that were caused on purpose. The banned-word check (`window.__vf.banned()`), an em-dash check and a "the the" check came back clean on 9 pages with every fold open: Lucknow, Nashik, Jokihat, Jaipur, Kotputli, Barshi keyword, Gadchiroli keyword, Port Blair and Gadchiroli emoc.
- LLM spend: none.
- Nothing was deployed, pushed or committed. The deployed app still runs the old build. Redeploying is the next stage's job.

## Low issues

Fixed:

- "the the" in the FRU substitution, and "no-traffic (no traffic, no waiting)". Paragraph spacing in How we decided. Screenshot: `fixed_lucknow_how_we_decided_text.png`.
- The blood-bank "Why ask" now reads "No fact in the record mentions a blood bank or transfusion. A C-section can need blood."
- Call button accessible name starts with the visible text ("Show number to call: Soni Hospital"). The why-not what-if buttons get a name, and their label is now "For planners: what if it were verified?".
- Error states have "Try again". The unknown-service message is "That service is not in the list. Pick one, or choose Something else and type it." If /api/sim fails, the page says "The village view could not load for this place."
- "From the middle of Centre of Chennai district" now reads "From the centre of Chennai district, Tamil Nadu (about 17 km across; ...)". "Lakshadweep District district" now reads "Lakshadweep district".
- The landing select shows "Emergency C-section (high-risk birth)". The full label is in the option's title. Screenshot: `fixed_landing_select_label.png`.
- The Jokihat ring label in the what-if view reads "1 hour: no real record here shows dialysis". Screenshot: `fixed_jokihat_whatif_ring_label.png`.
- Jokihat "about 2 min further" is now computed from unrounded minutes (89.74 and 91.18) and reads "about the same drive".
- The funnel shows "223 ... (222 distinct facilities; some records are duplicates)" (Kotputli).
- The sim row label is now "first stop has no usable evidence (none in its record, or held back by a warning)". When held-back first stops exist, the teaser sentence says the same.
- The what-if "Change" for the average drive is computed from unrounded values.
- The too-coarse coordinate headline now says "from the point you gave (read to about 111 km)", not "from the middle of the point you gave".

Open (not changed this round):

- Demo mode masks public hospital names too. Splitting name masking from phone masking changes `vf.why_not` masking; kept as D051 set it.
- PIN 742101 resolves to a round-number point (23.0004, 88.0005). This is a places-v3 data issue. Do not use it on camera.
- Landing example dots: only Kotputli is labeled.
- Jaipur markers 2 and 3 overlap. Jokihat marker 1 has a truncated label. The data has "Dinajpur Uttar".
- Kotputli and Kotputali get different origin framing. Ujala Physiotherapy Clinic is a C-section why-not row (strip selection rule). The place input has no inputmode hint.
- The lead quote "Head of Trauma Unit identified among listed doctors" (Kotputli) is still chosen.
- The Karakoram point 35.5,77.5 is called outside India (resolver border).
- The map canvas has no focus ring. Focus after picking an ambiguous place was not rechecked.
- The claims-policy run on Lucknow takes about 18 s, longer than the planned 15 s beat. "Which record should be checked first?" still needs a scroll at 900 px height. The funnel tiles cannot be clicked, so drop that beat from the script.
- A what-if average showing 28 to 28 where the auditor recomputed 27.47: only the change column was fixed. The engine's own average was not traced.
- Keyword-mode map legend wording (M16).
